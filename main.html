FAQs

What is RankFlow?

RankFlow is an all-in-one SEO platform for agencies. It builds WordPress sites, writes AI content and blogs, handles technical SEO, posts to Google Business Profile, runs review requests, tracks rankings, and delivers white-label reports.

Who is RankFlow for?

Agency teams that deliver websites, SEO, or blogging for clients. Team roles live in RankFlow, and a client login sees only that client's own site.

What can I deliver with it?

Three service types: a full website, SEO on an existing site, or blogs only. A full build includes home, service, location, about, contact, gallery, blog, 404, privacy, and terms pages.

How does GoHighLevel fit in?

Import clients from GoHighLevel or add them through an intake form. RankFlow includes a GHL onboarding checklist and snapshot loading, GHL form popups, and review request campaigns through GHL. The GHL Onboarding and GHL Cleaner Chrome extensions are part of the agency tools.

Will RankFlow overwrite edits I make in WordPress?

No. The self-updating plugin pushes pages, headers, footers, menus, fonts, and colors, and it protects edits made in WordPress from being overwritten.

What does a client see?

A white-label client portal with before-and-after site health and a log of work done, plus scheduled client reports. The client login is limited to that client's own site.

Where do rankings come from?

Rankings come from Google Search Console. Setup is one click, and new blogs are sent to Google, Bing, and IndexNow automatically.
