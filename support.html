FAQs

How do I book a RankFlow demo?

Email support@wealthlinkmedia.com with your name, agency, and the service type you want to see. The Book a RankFlow Demo button on the main page brings you here.

I submitted the form. Where is my access?

The form sends you to the RankFlow page. Use Get RankFlow Access there for the signup link. If that link is not open yet, email support and we will follow up at the address you entered.

Can a client log in?

Yes. RankFlow has agency team roles, plus a client login that sees only that client's own site, including the white-label portal and the log of work done.

What if I need help after I have access?

Email the same support address with your account email and a short description of the client or the page you are stuck on. Include a screenshot if the issue is visual.

Still need help?

Email support@wealthlinkmedia.com and include the email you used to request access or a demo.
